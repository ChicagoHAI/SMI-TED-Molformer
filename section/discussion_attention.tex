\para{Chemical structure is represented more strongly in hidden states than in attention.}
Although both models exhibit only weak alignment between attention and 3D molecular geometry, probing reveals that chemically meaningful properties are nevertheless strongly encoded within the residual stream, particularly in \smi. Properties such as ring membership, degree, and chirality achieve high probe accuracy despite the absence of strongly geometry-aligned attention heads. This suggests that chemically relevant structure is not represented primarily through explicit geometry-tracking attention patterns, but instead emerges in distributed hidden-state representations. The contrast is especially pronounced in \smi, where contextual chemical properties become highly recoverable by mid-depth, indicating that strong chemical representations can emerge even when attention itself shows only limited correspondence with spatial proximity.
